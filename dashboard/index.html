      Long: trend continuation

      1. Price above the 1H/D 200 EMA.
      2. Pullback to VWAP from above. Reclaim: dips below and closes back above. Bounce: low touches VWAP, bullish body closes above.
      3. Trigger volume ≥ 1.5 × Vol SMA(20).
      4. Buy the trigger close or a break of its high.

      Short: trend continuation

      1. Price below the 1H/D 200 EMA.
      2. Rally into VWAP from below. Breakdown: pokes above, closes back below. Rejection: wick tags VWAP, bearish close.
      3. Trigger volume ≥ 1.5 × Vol SMA(20).
      4. Short the trigger close or a break of its low.
